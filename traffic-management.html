Traffic Management Solutions

Intelligent, end-to-end traffic control systems that reduce congestion, improve safety, and optimize flow — built to perform in every condition.

Overview

Smarter Traffic Control for Every Road

Our traffic management portfolio covers the full spectrum — from basic fixed-time signals to advanced adaptive traffic control systems (ATCS) with real-time response. Every product is micro-controller based, built for extreme weather, and designed to minimize installation complexity.

Products operate at -20°C to 65°C, are dust and rain proof, and offer visibility up to 500-800 meters. All systems support remote monitoring and control via GSM, GPS, and Wi-Fi connectivity.

Traffic Management Products & Systems

Wireless Traffic Signals

Micro-controller based with GSM, GPS & Wi-Fi. Graphical timer, pedestrian signal, VIP/rescue mode. 300mm & 200mm polycarbonate aspects.

Adaptive Traffic Control (ATCS)

Real-time adaptive signals that respond to live traffic conditions, reducing congestion and average journey times significantly.

ANPR Systems

Automatic Number Plate Recognition for enforcement, toll collection, parking management, and vehicle tracking applications.

Countdown Timers

Fixed-time graphical countdown timers recommended for low-speed areas and high pedestrian traffic intersections.

Solar Traffic Blinker

12V DC solar-powered blinker with 24-48 hour battery backup. Battery capacity 7Ah to 18Ah, solar panel 20W to 40W.

Portable Traffic Signal

Height up to 3 meters, 12V DC, RED/AMBER/GREEN LEDs, dust and rain proof — perfect for construction zones and events.
